Mow Path: Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Mow Path: Lawn Mower Puzzle 3D (the "Game") handles and why. The Game is published on Google Play under the developer account livingexcellencegames by 33EXE STUDIOS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us").

In short: the Game has no in-app purchases. It sends crash reports and simple statistics about how levels are played, and some versions offer optional rewarded adverts. We never ask for your email, contacts, photos or location, the name you sign in the Game never leaves your phone, and we never sell your information.

1. Information kept on your device

Your progress (levels reached, stars and hints) and your settings (sound, music and vibration) are saved on your phone. At the start, the Game asks you to sign an agreement with a name of your choosing, which is used only to show your lawn care company's name in the Game. That name is stored only on your phone: it is never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and it is not part of any cloud save. All of this is removed when you uninstall the Game.

2. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID and display name so that cloud saves work. Your progress (levels, stars and hints, never your signed name or settings) can be stored in your Google account's saved games so it follows you to a new phone. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play the whole Game without signing in.

The Game includes Google's Play Games software for this, which on its own sends Google usage and diagnostic data about itself to keep the service stable. We don't receive that data.

3. Crash reports

If the Game crashes, a crash report is sent so we can find and fix the problem. It holds the technical state of the device and the Game at the time (for example, device model, Android version, app version and what the Game was doing), linked to a random installation ID, not your name or Google account. Crash reports reach us through Google Play's crash reporting and Google's Firebase Crashlytics, are processed by Google on our behalf (see Firebase privacy information), are used only to fix problems, and are kept for no longer than 90 days.

4. How the levels are played (analytics)

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to tell us which levels are too hard and where players stop, so we can improve them. It records simple game events: a level started, a level finished (with its stars, wasted moves, time and whether a hint was used), a hint taken, the clock running out and a new area reached. Firebase adds standard information on its own: a random app instance ID, the device model, Android version, app version, and your approximate country worked out from your IP address (not your precise location). The signed name is never included. We look at these statistics only in total, never to identify you, and they are kept for 14 months. Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for it first.

5. Rewarded adverts (optional)

In versions of the Game that offer them, you can choose to watch an advert to get extra hints. Adverts are served by Google AdMob and are always something you choose: the Game never shows an advert you did not ask for. To serve and measure adverts, AdMob uses your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier you can reset or delete in your phone's settings), device information and your approximate location from your IP address. This is handled by Google under the Google advertising policies. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent through Google's consent form before any personalised advert is shown, and you can change that choice later from the Game's settings.

6. What we don't do

  • No in-app purchases or payment information.
  • No adverts you didn't choose to watch.
  • No selling, renting or trading of any information.
  • No collection of your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • No sending of the name you sign in the Game.

7. Children

The Game's content is family-friendly, but the Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security and where data is stored

Saved games kept through Google Play Games, crash reports and analytics are stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country, and are protected by Google's security measures. Data sent by the Game is encrypted in transit. Nobody but Google receives it, and Google processes it on our behalf.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the Game removes everything stored on your phone, including your signed name.
  • You can remove the Game's Play Games data from your Google account at any time (Play Games app or myaccount.google.com).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask us to delete the crash reports and analytics linked to your installation by emailing us.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, or California's CCPA), you may have further rights, and we will honour them. Email us with any request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the version of the Game that the change is about is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes.
